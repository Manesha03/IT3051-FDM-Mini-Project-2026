Suggested Daily Workflow

  1. Morning: Check new bookings and flag high-risk reservations.

  4. Monthly: Discuss risk patterns with management and adjust follow-up rules.

About This System

This dashboard helps hotel staff review cancellation patterns and estimate the cancellation risk of a new reservation.

The prediction is a decision-support tool. Staff should still use normal guest communication and hotel policy when deciding follow-up actions.
